LIL AXOLOTL: TIDEKEEPER — The Living Watershed

Xochimilco is the only place on Earth where axolotls still live wild.
You are one. Clean it up.

A cozy 2D pixel platformer and swimmer about restoring a wetland one reach at a
time. Spray the oil, snap the leak shut, bring back the reeds — and the water
answers. Fish return. Vegetation spreads. The percentage in the corner climbs
because the ecosystem underneath it actually changed, not because you ticked a box.

Plays right here in your browser. 🌱


🌊 WHAT RESTORATION ACTUALLY MEANS HERE

There is no single "cleanliness" bar being filled. Each reach tracks five
ecological variables — purity, oxygen, clarity, invasive ratio and vegetation —
and every one of them moves for a reason:

• Purity rises as you clear oil barrels and cap the leak feeding the slick.
• Oxygen rises as debris and pests come out of the water.
• Clarity is held down by invasive fish for as long as they are in the reach.
• Vegetation grows over time — but ONLY while its conditions hold. Let them
  slip and it regresses. The watershed can go backwards, and it will show you.

Each reach has its own recipe for what "restored" means. Meeting it is what
opens the way on.


🐢 THE PARTY

You rescue them; they travel with you afterwards, and they each do one thing:

• TOLA — Mexican mud turtle. Shell Spin: a pilotable carve through rubble and
  the plugs sealing doors shut.
• MENO — Montezuma leopard frog. Tongue Snap, and a springboard — jump off his
  back for height you can't reach alone. He dives now, too.
• ZUNI — great darner dragonfly. Survey: a spiral sweep that reveals what's
  hidden in the reach, then hovers over the worst-oxygen point in it.
• NUTRIA — Neotropical river otter. She joins the party and travels with you,
  but her Herd and Haul verbs are still in development — her chip does nothing
  yet. She's here because the places she belongs to shipped first.

One partner is active at a time, and one shared button fires whatever that
partner does.


🕹️ HOW TO PLAY

• Move / swim — WASD or arrow keys
• Jump — SPACE     • Run — SHIFT     • Dash — X
• Spray — C
• Bubble Bomb — hold V to aim, release to blast a wide patch of oil
• Partner verb — Z
• Swap partner — Q
• Restart reach — R     • Menu — ESC


🗺️ FIVE REACHES

The canals, an estuary of reed marsh, the home embarcadero, and two quieter
sandboxes — a creek and a refugio — where Zuni and Nutria are found. They
connect through portals; some are plugged, and a plugged door is something Tola
can open.

⚠️ Honest warning: there is no map, minimap or compass in this build, and doors
carry no destination label. Getting lost is currently possible and it's the next
thing on the list. If you're turned around, every route eventually leads back to
the embarcadero.


🌱 WHERE THIS IS

An in-development build, not a finished game. Five reaches are playable end to
end and the restoration systems are live. Still coming: the otter's verbs, a
painted second canal reach, and wayfinding so you can see where you've been.

Made in Godot 4.7.
