# LIL AXOLOTL: TIDEKEEPER — The Living Watershed

### Xochimilco is the only place on Earth where axolotls still live wild. You are one. Clean it up.

A cozy 2D pixel platformer and swimmer about restoring a wetland one reach at a
time. Spray the oil, snap the leak shut, bring back the reeds — and the water
answers. Fish return. Vegetation spreads. The percentage in the corner climbs
because the ecosystem underneath it actually changed, not because you ticked a box.

**Plays right here in your browser.** 🌱

---

## 🌊 What restoration actually means here

There is no single "cleanliness" bar being filled. Each reach tracks five
ecological variables — **purity, oxygen, clarity, invasive ratio and
vegetation** — and every one of them moves for a reason:

- **Purity** rises as you clear oil barrels and cap the leak feeding the slick.
- **Oxygen** rises as debris and pests come out of the water.
- **Clarity** is held down by invasive fish for as long as they're in the reach.
- **Vegetation** grows over time — but *only* while its conditions hold. Let
  them slip and it regresses. The watershed can go backwards, and it will show you.

Each reach has its own recipe for what "restored" means. Meeting it opens the way on.

## 🐢 The party

You rescue them; they travel with you afterwards, and they each do one thing.

| Who | Species | Verb |
|---|---|---|
| **Tola** | Mexican mud turtle | **Shell Spin** — a pilotable carve through rubble and the plugs sealing doors shut |
| **Meno** | Montezuma leopard frog | **Tongue Snap**, plus a springboard — jump off his back for height you can't reach alone |
| **Zuni** | Great darner dragonfly | **Survey** — a spiral sweep revealing what's hidden, then a hover over the worst-oxygen point |
| **Nutria** | Neotropical river otter | Travels with you, but her Herd and Haul verbs are **still in development** |

One partner is active at a time, and one shared button fires whatever that partner does.

## 🗺️ Five reaches

The canals, an estuary of reed marsh, the home embarcadero, and two quieter
sandboxes — a creek and a refugio — where Zuni and Nutria are found. They
connect through portals; some are plugged, and a plugged door is something Tola
can open.

> ⚠️ **Honest warning:** there's no map, minimap or compass in this build, and
> doors carry no destination label. Getting lost is currently possible, and it's
> the next thing on the list. If you're turned around, every route eventually
> leads back to the embarcadero.

---

## 🌱 Where this is

An **in-development build**, not a finished game. Five reaches are playable end
to end and the restoration systems are live. Still coming: the otter's verbs, a
painted second canal reach, and wayfinding so you can see where you've been.

Made in **Godot 4.7**.
